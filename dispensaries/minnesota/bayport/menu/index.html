On the shelf

What the menu carries

Deli flower (weighed at the counter, priced by the gram), packaged flower (pre-packs), pre-rolls, vapes and carts, THC edibles, hemp edibles, hemp THC beverages, hemp topicals, cannabis seeds, and accessories. The menu changes with each batch; check https://mydealermn.com/bayport for what's on the shelf today.

Brands on the live menu include Vireo, RYTHM, Dogwalkers, Good Green, Campfire Cannabis, and Trawa. The lineup changes; the live menu is the source of truth.

Before you go

Up to 2 ounces of flower, 8 grams of concentrate (vapes count here), and 800 mg of THC in edibles (shared across cannabis edibles, hemp edibles, and hemp THC drinks) per purchase.

My Dealer takes cash, or your debit card through the cashless ATM. There is also a regular ATM on site. The cashless ATM has a $3 fee and runs in $10 steps, so your withdrawal rounds up to the nearest $10 and you get the difference back in cash.
