Getting here from Stillwater

From downtown and Main Street

Head south on Main Street, which is Highway 95. Past the edge of downtown it becomes St. Croix Trail. Stay on 95 through the Highway 36 interchange and keep going south. When you pass the Andersen campus on the north end of Bayport, you're close: 95 turns into 3rd Street and we're on your left, next to the American Legion and the post office, across from City Hall.

About 5 minutes, 3.1 miles.

From west Stillwater and Highway 36

If you're up on the Highway 36 side of town, take 36 east to the Highway 95 exit (Stillwater / Bayport) and turn south. From the exit it's about a mile and a half, around 2 minutes. Store on your left.

One tip: Bayport goes by fast. Our sign sits flat on the brick, so look for the face with the hat.

Drive time and distance: OpenStreetMap routing (OSRM), checked October 22, 2026, without traffic. Allow extra time when traffic is heavy.

Parking and getting in

Parking is easy. There's a paved parking lot behind the building, with a second entrance at the back, so you can park and walk right in. To get to the back lot, turn onto 1st Ave N, then take a left down the alley. There's also street parking out front on 3rd Street and a public lot at City Hall across the street.

You'll show your ID when you get to the counter. Plan on a quick stop if you preordered, and a little longer if you want to talk it through with a budtender.

What we carry

We're a full adult-use cannabis store, licensed by the Minnesota Office of Cannabis Management (license DIS-L24-000241). The flower is grown as cannabis by licensed Minnesota cultivators, and anything hemp-derived is labeled as hemp so you know which is which.

Flower is the heart of the shop. You can get deli flower weighed out by the gram at the counter, where the per-gram price drops as you buy more, or grab a sealed 3.5 g or 7 g pre-pack. Past that, there are pre-rolls in singles and packs, edibles (licensed cannabis gummies and hemp-derived ones), all-in-one vapes, and hemp THC drinks, plus hemp topicals, seeds, and accessories.

There's a lot of variety on the shelves, too, with a wide range of brands, many of them from Minnesota. Brands we carry include RYTHM, Dogwalkers, Good Green, &Shine, Vireo, Campfire Cannabis, Flör, BLNCD, Minny Grown, Foundry, Granny's, and Philotimo, plus Trawa flower at the deli counter. The lineup changes as new batches come in.

Stock turns over as new batches come in, so check the flower menu or the full live menu before you head down. If you already know what you want, preorder at mydealermn.com/bayport and it'll be waiting.

Deli flower, about 5 minutes from Stillwater

If you've been looking for a dispensary near Stillwater with budget-friendly flower, this is the part of the shop I'm proudest of. Deli flower is weighed to order in front of you at the counter on certified scales, so you buy exactly as much as you want, from a single gram up to two ounces, the Minnesota limit for one purchase.

Mix and match strains within a price tier and the combined weight counts toward the bulk price, which puts ounces at $210 or $225. The more you buy, the less each gram costs. In the $11 tier, a single gram is $11, 3.5 g works out to $10.00 a gram, 14 g to $8.57 a gram, and a full ounce to $7.50 a gram. The $12 tier steps down the same way, to $8.04 a gram at the ounce. From Main Street it's about 5 minutes down Highway 95.

First visit

Bring a valid photo ID. Everyone has to be 21 or older, every visit, and nobody under 21 can come in with you. Any U.S. state license or ID works, so out-of-town guests staying in Stillwater are fine, and so do passports, Tribal IDs, and Canadian IDs.

We take cash, or debit through our cashless ATM ($3 fee). No credit cards. There's a regular ATM inside too. Totals round up to the nearest dollar after tax, and that roundup is the staff tip, so there's nothing extra to add.

Give us your phone number at checkout to join the loyalty program. You earn 3% back in points, and most of our promotions are for members. Veterans and active military get 15% off every day, and Andersen employees get 10% off on Tuesdays.

Minnesota caps a single purchase at 2 ounces of flower, 8 grams of concentrate, and 800 mg of THC in edibles and hemp THC products combined.

You can shop three ways: talk it through with a budtender, browse on the in-store kiosk, or preorder online. If you're new, start with a budtender. They'll walk you through labels and formats without the sales pitch.

Local rules: Stillwater City Code Sec. 12-125 (Ordinance 1204, adopted August 2, 2023) bans using cannabis or hemp products in public places, meaning anywhere generally open to the public, including City parks, trails, sidewalks, and City parking lots. A violation is a petty misdemeanor. Stillwater questions

Is My Dealer in Stillwater?

No, we're in downtown Bayport at 243 3rd St N, about 3 miles south of downtown Stillwater on Highway 95. From Main Street it's about 5 minutes without traffic.

Can I order ahead from Stillwater and pick up on the way?

Yes. Preorder at mydealermn.com/bayport and pick up the same day or the next day. You pay at pickup and show your ID at the counter. Orders not picked up by the end of the next day go back into inventory.

Where do I park?

The building has entrances at both the front and the back, so come in whichever way is easier. There's a paved lot behind the building: turn onto 1st Ave N, then take a left down the alley. Street parking on 3rd Street out front and the public lot at City Hall across the street also work.

Can I use it in downtown Stillwater, on the riverfront, or in my car?

No. Minnesota law doesn't allow use in public places like parks and sidewalks, or in any motor vehicle, parked or moving. That includes our parking lot. Use it at home or on private property where the owner allows it. More on where it's legal.

Do you deliver to Stillwater?

Not right now. It's in-store shopping or preorder for pickup. If that changes, we'll post it on the FAQ.
