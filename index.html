How to shop

Three ways to shop.

  1. I

    In store, in person

    Feel free to browse the menu in advance for inspiration. You are welcome to chat with a budtender in the store and fine tune the purchase on the sales floor. We also have paper menus for people who don't like screens or technology.

  2. II

    In store, on an iPad kiosk

    The hybrid approach if you prefer to shop alone and didn't plan ahead. See the live menu, create a cart, and have it fulfilled while you are in the store. QR codes make it easy to pull the menu up on your phone.

  3. III

    Online preorder for pickup

    Place your order now to reserve the inventory for your arrival. This is the quick in and out if you are passing by and already know what you want. Pick up your preorder the same day or the next day only. Orders not picked up by the end of the next day are released back to inventory.

My Dealer

You already have a dealer. We'd like that to be us.

The cannabis market used to be straightforward but has quickly become noisy and confusing for customers. Neon lit tobacco shops and gas stations are selling THC delta 6a, 8, 9, 10, THC-O, HHC, THCP, exo-THC and THCA in states where cannabis isn't even legalized. It's confusing... What happened to the old weed we all knew?! We also still have a black market operating that works because it is inexpensive, craft, and already in your network, but the service, accessibility, and product variety might not be A+. My Dealer is the alternative. A shop you can walk into with transparency and truth, a menu that actually changes, and is grown as cannabis here in Minnesota.

The alley

Your guy

Your guy is usually pretty good. The catch is the menu. Most growers have a strain or two and they have to sell through what they grew. You meet when they can meet. You take what they have. Same flower, over and over, until the next run.

The hemp shop

The costume

Tobacco shops calling themselves dispensaries. THCA flower. Hemp grown as hemp, then converted. Sometimes sprayed. Legal in a loophole kind of way. Confusing on purpose.

My Dealer

The real thing

Walk in when you want. Check ID. Pick from a real menu. Edibles today, Blue Dream tomorrow, a different preroll next week. Grown as cannabis by licensed Minnesota cultivators. You can send grandma in for a tincture and still tell your friends you are going to your dealer to pick up an ounce of flower.
