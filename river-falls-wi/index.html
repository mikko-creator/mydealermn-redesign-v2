First, the rule: buy in Minnesota, use it in Minnesota

I'd rather lose a sale than have you get in trouble, so here it is up front. What you buy at My Dealer is legal in Minnesota only, and it doesn't go back down I-94 toward River Falls. Wisconsin treats THC (outside of legal hemp) as a controlled substance, and the St. Croix County part of River Falls is also covered by a county possession ordinance. Every package we sell carries the line "This product may be unlawful outside the state of Minnesota."

Before you make the drive, sort out where you'll use it on this side of the river, like a private residence or private property where the owner is fine with it. Our guide for Wisconsin shoppers has the statutes and the rest of the detail.

Local rules around River Falls

River Falls sits in two counties. In the St. Croix County part of town, county Chapter 58 bans marijuana possession (up to 25 grams, first offense), with a forfeiture of $125 to $500. St. Croix County ch. 58 (PDF) Pierce County's code (Chapter 176, Article II) prohibits marijuana possession, with a forfeiture of up to $500 for an ounce or less. Hemp THC on December 11: what changes, and what doesn't

Heads up for Wisconsin shoppers looking for cannabis near River Falls. Under current federal law, starting December 11, 2026, a finished hemp product with more than 0.4 mg of total THC per package (the can, bottle, or bag it's sold in) no longer meets the federal definition of hemp. So do hemp products made with lab-converted cannabinoids. That covers most hemp THC gummies, vapes, and drinks, including delta-8. This is a federal change, not a new Wisconsin law. Wisconsin's hemp law hasn't changed, and two Wisconsin bills to regulate hemp THC products, AB 747 and SB 682, did not pass.

Minnesota has its own hemp law. The Minnesota Office of Cannabis Management says the federal change doesn't change how Minnesota defines lower-potency hemp edibles and drinks, or whether they're legal under Minnesota law. OCM also notes that many hemp products that comply with Minnesota law, such as packages with more than 0.4 mg of THC, won't meet the new federal definition, which could affect hemp businesses' banking, taxes, and interstate shipping.

Licensed Minnesota cannabis was never hemp. It's what most people mean by real weed: grown as cannabis by licensed Minnesota cultivators, tested, and tracked by the state. It's legal under Minnesota law only, and it's still illegal under federal law and in Wisconsin. Come visit Bayport, about 25 to 30 minutes from River Falls. Everything you buy here, cannabis or hemp, is for use in Minnesota only.

Where Wisconsin cannabis law stands

As of October 22, 2026, Wisconsin has no adult-use or medical cannabis program. In the 2025-2026 session, lawmakers introduced a medical cannabis bill, a legalization bill, and several bills to regulate hemp THC products. None passed; all failed on March 23, 2026, under the session schedule, after the last regular floor period ended in mid-March. River Falls questions

Can I bring it back to River Falls?

No. Cannabis isn't legal in Wisconsin, and taking it across the river is illegal under state law. The part of River Falls in St. Croix County is also covered by the county's possession ordinance (ch. 58), with a $125 to $500 forfeiture. The Pierce County side has a county possession ordinance (ch. 176) with a forfeiture of up to $500. Buy in Minnesota, use it in Minnesota. Details and sources are on our Wisconsin shoppers page.

Is there a licensed dispensary in River Falls?

No. Wisconsin has no adult-use or medical cannabis program, so River Falls has no state-licensed dispensary. Shops there that use the word dispensary sell hemp or CBD products. The licensed option is on the Minnesota side, in Bayport.

If a "dispensary near me" search in River Falls turns up hemp shops, that's because Wisconsin still has no licensed cannabis stores.
